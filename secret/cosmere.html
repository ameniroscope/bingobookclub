The Cosmere

So what is the Cosmere?

It is the shared universe behind most of Brandon Sanderson's epic fantasy. Many of his series happen on different planets within the same greater setting, so magic, history and the occasional traveller quietly connect them behind the scenes.

Quick test: if a book mentions Earth, it isn't Cosmere. The Wheel of Time (which Brandon finished for Robert Jordan) and his children's books, like Alcatraz, aren't Cosmere either.

The five-minute lore

The connective tissue, in broad strokes. It's fun background, not homework.

Adonalsium

Long before any of the books, a single vast power called Adonalsium shaped the worlds of the Cosmere. Think of it as the source everything else comes from.

The Shattering

A group of people conspired to kill Adonalsium and split its power between them. That event is called the Shattering, and it set the whole universe in motion.

The sixteen Shards

The power broke into sixteen pieces, the Shards, each a raw idea like Preservation, Honor, Cultivation or Ruin. Whoever holds a Shard is bent toward its nature.

The three Realms

Reality has three layers: the Physical (the ordinary world), the Cognitive (a realm shaped by thought, where spirits of ideas live), and the Spiritual (where souls and connections are, outside time).

Investiture

Investiture is the raw stuff of magic, the power of the Shards taking a form people can use. Every magic system in the Cosmere is Investiture wearing a different set of rules.

You don't need any of this to enjoy a single Cosmere book. Every series stands completely on its own; the deeper connections are a bonus you can chase later, or never. Start anywhere that sounds fun.
