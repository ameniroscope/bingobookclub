Our next read swaps wands-as-usual for wands-as-science...

Same wizarding world, very different Harry — this one was raised by a biochemist professor, read way too much science before age eleven, and treats "magic just works that way" as a personal insult. He's here to run experiments, question everything, and probably get several professors quietly worried about him.

Yes, it's fanfiction. It's also 660,000+ words that turn a children's fantasy series into a rationalist fever dream — equal parts funny, unsettling, and weirdly educational. Bring your skepticism, your patience for footnote-brained protagonists, and your willingness to side-eye Quirrell from page one.
