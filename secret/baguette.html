Baguette Express

  1. Mix 250 ml of warm water with 10 g of fresh baker's yeast (5 g if dried).
  2. Let sit for 1 min, then mix thoroughly.
  3. Add 8 g of salt and 320 g of flour. Mix well — no need to knead.
  4. Let rise 1 h 30 under a damp cloth (it needs to touch the dough).
  5. Shape into rolls, cover again, and let rise 30 min.
  6. In an oven pre-heated to 240 °C, add the dough and empty a glass of water into the drip tray.
  7. Bake for 25 min.
